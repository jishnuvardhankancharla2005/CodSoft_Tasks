Model notes
Feature importance
Why an SVM

Six algorithms were compared with 10-fold stratified cross-validation (logistic regression, KNN, SVM, decision tree, random forest). A linear-kernel SVM generalised best, and its exact learned weights are embedded directly in this page — every prediction above comes from the real trained model, not an approximation.

Where errors happen

Setosa is linearly separable and classified with 100% accuracy. Almost all model error occurs between versicolor and virginica, whose petal measurements overlap at the boundary — visible in the dataset itself, not a model weakness.
